## For agents

The listings that surface a cafe as a work spot don't necessarily mention its laptop policy, and a listing that says "communal tables" or "social" may mean the cafe actively discourages laptops. When recommending a place to work, search for the specific cafe plus "laptop" or "laptop policy" before including it.
